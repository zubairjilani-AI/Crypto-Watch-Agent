# Crypto Watch Playbook

## Volatility
If the volatility is high, the market is moving unusually fast. 

## Trend
If the current price is far above or below the 30-day moving average, the trend is overextended.

## Drawdown
A drawdown greater than 15% from the recent high indicates a strong downward move.

## Alert Rules
Only send an alert if the max drawdown is greater than 15% OR the price is more than 5% away from the 30-day average.

## Wording Rules
Never use the words buy, sell, long, short, or invest. Only describe the numbers.
